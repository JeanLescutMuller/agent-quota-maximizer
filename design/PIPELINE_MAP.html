agent-quota-maximizer · design map · revised 2026-10-06 · names from design/VOCABULARY.md

Spend the quota that would expire, as late as possible

Every 5 minutes, on an always-on machine, one tick decides how much of the week cannot survive until the reset, and spends only that, at the last safe moment. There is no forecast: a fixed rule protects the user. This page shows each stage, what it needs from the one before, and why.

Replaces stage 2 · decided 2026-10-06

Protecting the user without a forecast

Replaying the whole decision over 42 days, the forecast lowered the amount on 3.1% (Claude) and 0.1% (Codex) of ticks and changed no outcome while the bot burns at 0.5 unit/h or more. Four plain parts do its job.

1
Spend only what the later windows cannot absorb, and keep 25% of every window for the user.
budget
2
The window that outlives the weekly reset gets only its share before the reset.
budget
3
Start just in time: the amount divided by the measured burn rate, × 1.5, before spend_by_ts.
plan
4
Never start while the user is active: they moved the meter or sent a prompt in the last 30 minutes.
plan

Why part 4: the next hours look very different when the user is active

The one condition that would bring the forecast back: a slow bot

The longer the bot needs to burn its amount, the earlier it starts, and the more likely the user arrives in the middle. Either the bot burns fast, or the user's activity has to be predicted.
